## Brand & Style

This design system channels the timeless prestige of lawn tennis, the manicured rigor of Centre Court, and the clarity required for elite sport education. The emotional tone is poised, authoritative, and welcoming—merging quintessential sporting tradition with contemporary digital lucidity.

The visual style is **Contemporary Editorial Minimalism with Heritage Craft**:
- **White-Line Precision**: Crisp division inspired by court chalk, ultra-refined borders, and expansive whitespace reflecting morning grass courts.
- **Editorial Distinction**: High-contrast, stately serif headings establish historical weight and grand slam pedigree, offset by razor-sharp, functional sans-serifs engineered for rule breakdowns and tactical diagrams.
- **Controlled Opulence**: Deep Wimbledon pine greens paired with warm champagne brass accents evoke silverware, clubhouses, and sunlit grandstands, steering clear of artificial neon gradients while maintaining modern athletic legibility.

## Colors

The palette balances historical heritage with athletic clarity, using dedicated court colors for tournament categorization and high-legibility neutrals for deep instructional reading.

### Palette Overview
- **Primary (`#0E4D2B`)**: Deep Wimbledon Lawn Green. Used for primary navigation, primary action buttons, key banners, and flagship editorial headers.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059`)**: Heritage Trophy Gold. Deployed sparingly for laurels, achievement badges, highlights, verified credentials, and active tab indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#2E7D32`)**: Active Court Green. Used for active states, interactive success cues, positive scoring indicators, and secondary accent buttons.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1C2826`)**: Deep Turf Slate. Used for primary body text, titles on light backgrounds, and high-emphasis icons. Sub-headings use `#2D3748`.
- **Canvas & Surfaces**:
  - Base Lawn Canvas: `#F8FAF8` (crisp, tinted off-white).
  - Elevated Card Background: `#FFFFFF`.
  - Secondary Container / Well: `#F1F5F2` (subtle mint-limestone tint).

### Tournament Domain Accents
For tournament discovery and slam-specific labeling, rely on standard tokens:
- **Wimbledon Grass**: `#0E4D2B` with `#E8F2EC` surface tint.
- **Roland Garros Clay**: `#C35831` with `#FBEFEA` surface tint.
- **Australian Open Cyan Hardcourt**: `#0082CA` with `#E5F4FC` surface tint.
- **US Open DecoTurf Blue**: `#1A3B8B` with `#E8EDF8` surface tint.
- **Optic Ball Accent**: `#7CB342` reserved for interactive tooltips, progress bars, and diagram points of interest.

## Typography

The typography couples the scholarly distinction of **EB Garamond** with the clean geometry and athletic utility of **Hanken Grotesk**.

- **Editorial Serifs (EB Garamond)**: Expressly utilized for Grand Slam titles, rule headers, historical excerpts, quotes from the Code of Conduct, and major feature banners. It gives learning materials an archival, certified distinction.
- **Modern Performance Sans (Hanken Grotesk)**: Powers interface controls, rule clause breakdowns, visual diagram callouts, interactive quizzes, and micro-metrics.
- **Uppercase Hierarchy**: `label-caps` is always rendered in full uppercase with tracking (`0.08em`) to delineate categories such as court surface, fault types (e.g., "FOOT FAULT", "LET", "CHALLENGE"), and tournament seed numbers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a structured, fluid 12-column grid on desktop, transitioning to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

- **Grid Alignment**: Emulate the geometry of a tennis court—symmetrical, framed, and measured. Content blocks conform strictly to grid lines, keeping maximum reading widths on educational narratives bounded to 680px for sustained focus.
- **Rhythm & White Lines**: Section divides rely on generous vertical spacing combined with hairline borders (`1px solid #E2E8E4`) reminiscent of court boundary lines.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Mobile` (< 640px): 4-column layout, dense vertical card stacking, full-width violation charts, horizontal swipe carousels for Grand Slam badges.
  - `Tablet` (640px – 1024px): 8-column layout, 2-column card pairs for rule-by-rule comparisons.
  - `Desktop` (≥ 1025px): 12-column layout, multi-pane exploration (e.g., persistent rule navigation on the left, visual court breakdown in the center, memory tips and penalty escalation on the right).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **tonal layering and low-contrast chalk boundaries** rather than heavy drop shadows, reinforcing the manicured grass court atmosphere.

- **Level 0 (Court Bed)**: The base canvas is `#F8FAF8`.
- **Level 1 (Surface Containers / Cards)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` outlined with a calibrated hairline border `1px solid rgba(14, 77, 43, 0.08)`. 
- **Level 2 (Active & Floating Modules)**: Modals, interactive dropdowns, and flyout violation explanations introduce an ambient, warm grass shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(14, 77, 43, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(28, 40, 38, 0.04)`.
- **Inverted Focus**: Key educational callouts and historical trivia utilize deep forest container blocks (`#0E4D2B`) with crisp gold text elements, anchoring the eye downwards on pivotal concepts.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates **Soft (Level 1)** geometry. 

Elements use a subtle, tailored corner radius (`4px` / `0.25rem`) that echoes traditional leather-bound club books, trophy plinths, and official rule sheets:
- Standard buttons, text inputs, violation step cards, and court diagram tooltips feature `4px` corner radii.
- Surface cards and full-width diagram containers expand to `rounded-lg` (`8px` / `0.5rem`).
- Only pill badges (e.g., Grand Slam status chips, score tags, set counters) utilize rounded pill terminations (`9999px`) to match the circular contour of tennis balls.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Button**: Deep green fill (`#0E4D2B`), crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), `4px` radius, subtle gold border on active hover (`1px solid #C5A059`).
- **Secondary Button**: Ivory/white fill, `1px solid #0E4D2B`, dark green text. Hover shifts to `#F1F5F2`.
- **Heritage Action**: Gold outline (`#C5A059`), dark charcoal text, ideal for unlocking certified rules, grand slam archives, or specialized masterclasses.

### Grand Slam Badges & Chips
- Compact pill-shaped elements (`9999px` radius) with `label-caps` typography:
  - **Wimbledon**: Background `#E8F2EC`, border `1px solid #0E4D2B`, text `#0E4D2B`.
  - **Roland Garros**: Background `#FBEFEA`, border `1px solid #C35831`, text `#C35831`.
  - **Australian Open**: Background `#E5F4FC`, border `1px solid #0082CA`, text `#0082CA`.
  - **US Open**: Background `#E8EDF8`, border `1px solid #1A3B8B`, text `#1A3B8B`.

### Rule Cards & Infographic Modules
- **Rule Anatomy Card**: White container, `8px` radius, hairline border `rgba(14, 77, 43, 0.1)`. Features a distinct left accent line (`3px`) tinted in `#2E7D32` for legal play or `#C35831` for faults and infractions.
- **Memory Tip Callout ("Umpire's Note")**: Elevated panel with `#F1F5F2` fill, featuring a subtle heritage champagne quote icon and an italicized serif note summarizing complex rulings (e.g., "Hindrance", "Dry Rub vs. Wet Ball").

### Violation Breakdowns
- **Code Violation Progression Ladder**: Step-based horizontal or vertical timeline tracking Warning → Point Penalty → Game Penalty → Default. Uses subtle optic ball green indicators (`#7CB342`) for the current stage and high-contrast charcoal for penalty definitions.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Text Inputs**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #CFD8D3`, shifting to `1.5px solid #0E4D2B` on focus with no noisy halo glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Square-cut with `2px` corners; checked states display in deep green `#0E4D2B` with a sharp gold `#C5A059` check icon for high contrast.

### Lists & Key-Value Pairings
- Structured scorecards and glossary terms: Hairline horizontal separator (`#E2E8E4`) with uppercase `label-md` keys aligned left and serif values aligned right.
